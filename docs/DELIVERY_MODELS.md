# CampusEdge Training Delivery Models

To cater to diverse institutional goals, academic schedules, and student demographics, CampusEdge offers **three structured delivery packages**. Each package includes standardized instructor-led training, proctored hands-on coding labs, diagnostic assessments, continuous TPO reporting, and verified certifications.

## Package 1: Campus Foundation (120 Hours)

### Purpose & Objective
Designed for colleges looking to build strong computational thinking, eliminate coding phobia, and establish basic quantitative reasoning early in the 2nd and early 3rd year.

### Structure & Modules
1. **Programming & Linear DSA (60 Hours)**:
   - Syntax fluency & debugging in Java / C++ / Python.
   - Time & space complexity fundamentals.
   - Arrays, Strings, Searching, Sorting, Two-Pointers, and Singly/Doubly Linked Lists.
   - Stacks, Queues, and basic recursion.
2. **Foundational Aptitude (40 Hours)**:
   - Number systems, percentages, ratios, time-speed-distance, time-work.
   - Blood relations, direction sense, syllogisms, and coding-decoding.
3. **Spoken English & Professional Basics (20 Hours)**:
   - Eliminating mother tongue influence (MTI), body language, and professional email writing.

### Deliverables & Reports to College
- **Bi-Weekly Attendance & Performance Sheet**: Delivered in Excel to the TPO and Department HoDs.
- **Remedial Identification List**: Highlights the bottom 25% students needing extra lab support.
- **Course Completion Certificate**: Issued with QR code verification for NAAC Criterion 5.1.3 audit.

## Package 2: Placement-Ready Accelerator (200 Hours) — *Our Flagship Program*

### Purpose & Objective
Our most popular comprehensive package. It takes students through the entire recruitment gauntlet, ensuring they clear online coding tests and technical interviews for both mass recruiters (TCS, Infosys, Cognizant, Wipro, Accenture) and Tier-2 product/fintech firms (Capgemini, Virtusa, Hexaware, Persistent, LTI Mindtree).

### Structure & Modules
1. **Advanced DSA & Problem Solving (96 Hours)**:
   - Complete 16-week DSA syllabus (Arrays to DP, Trees, Graphs, Tries, Heaps, Disjoint Set).
   - 200+ curated LeetCode-grade problems solved in class and labs.
2. **Comprehensive Aptitude & Data Interpretation (64 Hours)**:
   - Complete coverage of Quant, Logical Reasoning, and Verbal Ability.
   - Company-specific test series (TCS NQT, Infosys DSY, Cognizant GenC Next, Accenture Critical Reasoning).
3. **Corporate Soft Skills, GD & 1-on-1 Mock (40 Hours)**:
   - ATS-optimized resume building with personalized 1-on-1 review.
   - Group discussion drills with live feedback.
   - Behavioral interview prep (STAR method) + **one 30-minute 1-on-1 mock interview** per enrolled student conducted by external industry panelists.

### Deliverables & Reports to College
- **Live Institutional TPO Dashboard**: 24/7 access to real-time student attendance, coding test scores, and submission frequency.
- **Weekly Monday Morning Executive MIS**: Summary email sent directly to the Principal and TPO highlighting attendance % and top/bottom performers.
- **Company Eligibility Predictive Index (CEPI)**: Detailed diagnostic tagging each student as "Ready for 7+ LPA", "Ready for 4-7 LPA", or "Needs Remediation".
- **NAAC / NBA Accreditation Dossier**: Pre-formatted data tables showing entry vs. exit assessment improvement delta, attendance logs, and student feedback ratings.

## Package 3: Premium Mentorship & Product Incubator (300 Hours + 1-on-1)

### Purpose & Objective
Engineered for autonomous engineering colleges and institutions aiming to elevate their median compensation packages and secure 12+ LPA to 30+ LPA offers from top-tier product companies (Amazon, Cisco, Microsoft, Walmart, Oracle, PhonePe, Razorpay, Goldman Sachs, and high-growth startups).

### Structure & Modules
1. **Competitive Programming & Advanced Algorithms (140 Hours)**:
   - Advanced Graph Algorithms (Shortest paths, MST, bridges, articulation points).
   - Complex DP (Bitmask DP, Digit DP, DP on trees).
   - Advanced Data Structures (Segment Trees, Fenwick Trees, Suffix Automata).
2. **System Design & Core CS Fundamentals (40 Hours)**:
   - Low-Level Design (LLD): Object-oriented design patterns, SOLID principles, UML schemas.
   - High-Level Design (HLD): Microservices, caching strategies (Redis), load balancing, relational vs. NoSQL databases, message queues (Kafka).
   - Core CS: Operating Systems (Threads, Deadlocks, Virtual Memory), DBMS (Indexing, Transactions, ACID), Computer Networks.
3. **Advanced Aptitude & Analytical Reasoning (60 Hours)**:
   - High-difficulty data interpretation, critical reasoning, and algorithmic puzzles.
4. **Intensive Interview Grooming & 1-on-1 Mentorship (60 Hours)**:
   - **Three full 45-minute 1-on-1 mock interviews** per student (2 Technical DSA/System Design + 1 Behavioral/Bar-Raiser).
   - GitHub open-source project code reviews and portfolio deployment.

### Deliverables & Reports to College
- **Executive Board Report**: Comprehensive placement forecasting presented directly to the Governing Body / Board of Trustees.
- **Direct Corporate Referral Pipeline**: CampusEdge connects the top performers with hiring managers in our partner network of product firms.
- **CampusEdge Honours Certificate**: Distinction credential recognizing top 5% percentiles.

## Operational Logistics & Infrastructure Requirements

To deliver an uninterrupted learning experience, the host college provides:
1. **Computer Laboratories**: 1 desktop/laptop per student with reliable broadband (minimum 50 Mbps dedicated line), Java 17+, Python 3.11, GCC/G++ 11+ or modern browser access for cloud IDEs.
2. **Audio-Visual Facility**: High-definition projector, collar mic, and whiteboard for lecture rooms.
3. **Dedicated Single Point of Contact (SPOC)**: A departmental faculty coordinator to ensure lab access and student attendance compliance.
4. **CampusEdge Operations Lead**: CampusEdge stations an on-site or virtual Program Coordinator to manage daily attendance, software setup, trainer logistics, and student queries.
