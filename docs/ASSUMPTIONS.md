## 5. Academic Calendars & Delivery Windows

Indian engineering colleges operate under rigid university examination calendars. Training interventions are planned around two distinct operational cycles:

1. **Integrated Semester Mode (Even & Odd Semesters)**:
   - **Odd Semester**: July to November (ideal for 3rd and final years before campus recruitment starts).
   - **Even Semester**: January to May (ideal for 2nd and 3rd years to build deep fundamentals).
   - **Time Commitment**: 4 to 6 hours per week (2 hours on Wednesday/Saturday afternoons, or dedicated zero-hour periods, or weekend sessions).
2. **Intensive Vacation Bootcamps**:
   - **Summer Bootcamp (May – July)**: 6 to 8 hours daily over 10 to 15 days immediately preceding the August campus hiring season.
   - **Winter Bootcamp (December – January)**: Intensive 7-day crash course during semester breaks.

## 6. Accreditation Alignment (NBA & NAAC)

Training programs are explicitly mapped to accreditation criteria so colleges can directly import CampusEdge reports into their Self-Assessment Reports (SAR):

- **NBA (National Board of Accreditation)**:
  - **Criterion 4**: Students' Performance (Enrolment, Placement Ratio, Median Package, Higher Studies).
  - **Criterion 5**: Faculty and Student Continuous Improvement Initiatives.
  - **Program Outcomes (POs)**:
    - `PO1` (Engineering Knowledge): Algorithmic fundamentals and computational complexity.
    - `PO2` (Problem Analysis): Formulating mathematical and logical solutions for complex data problems.
    - `PO3` (Design/Development of Solutions): Implementing robust, memory-safe, optimized code.
    - `PO9` (Individual and Team Work): Group discussions, collaborative coding, and paired problem solving.
    - `PO10` (Communication): Technical writing, resume drafting, interview articulating, and professional email etiquette.
    - `PO12` (Life-long Learning): Independent competitive programming and continuous skill upgrading.
- **NAAC (National Assessment and Accreditation Council)**:
  - **Criterion 5.1.3**: Capacity enhancement and development schemes (Soft skills, Language and communication skills, Life skills, Computing and technical skills).
  - **Criterion 5.2.1**: Percentage of placement of outgoing students and progression to higher education.

## 7. Commercial & Tax Assumptions

- **Currency**: Indian Rupees (INR / ₹).
- **Goods and Services Tax (GST)**:
  - Applicable GST rate: **18%**.
  - Service Accounting Code (SAC): **999293** (*Commercial training and coaching services*).
  - Invoices clearly separate base fee and 18% GST (CGST 9% + SGST 9% for intra-state, IGST 18% for inter-state).
- **Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)**:
  - Colleges typically deduct TDS under **Section 194J** (Fees for Professional/Technical Services at 10% or 2% as applicable) or **Section 194C** (Contractor services at 1% or 2%).
- **Payment Milestones**:
  - Standard split: **30% Advance on signing of MoU/Work Order**, **40% at Mid-point Evaluation**, and **30% within 15 days of program completion** and final report submission.
- **Pilot Assurance**:
  - Discounted or refundable pilot models: A 2-week pilot fee of ₹25,000 is 100% credited against the full semester contract upon formal onboarding.
