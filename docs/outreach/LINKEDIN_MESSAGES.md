# CampusEdge LinkedIn Outreach Playbook (TPO & Dean Targeting)

LinkedIn is an exceptionally effective channel to connect directly with college Training & Placement Officers (TPOs), Deans of Career Services, and Principals without being blocked by campus administrative gatekeepers.

---

## 1. Connection Request Notes (300 Characters Max)

### Option A: Peer-Benchmarking Angle (High Acceptance)
