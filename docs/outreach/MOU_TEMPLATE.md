### 1. PURPOSE & SCOPE OF ENGAGEMENT
1.1 The purpose of this MoU is to establish a collaborative institutional framework wherein CampusEdge shall deliver company-standard technical and placement readiness training for the eligible engineering/degree students of the Institution.  
1.2 The training shall encompass:
- **Technical Track**: Data Structures, Algorithms, and Object-Oriented Problem Solving in Java / C++ / Python.
- **Aptitude Track**: Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, and Verbal Ability.
- **Soft Skills & Corporate Finishing Track**: Professional Communication, ATS Resume Building, Group Discussions, and 1-on-1 Mock Technical & HR Interviews.

### 3. ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES OF CAMPUSEDGE
3.1 Deploy certified, experienced lead instructors with industry pedigrees to conduct scheduled lecture and lab sessions as per the agreed syllabus.  
3.2 Provide teaching assistants (TAs) to ensure a minimum 1:30 trainer/TA-to-student ratio during hands-on programming labs.  
3.3 Provide access to the CampusEdge Online Coding & Assessment Platform, diagnostic test series, and learning courseware.  
3.4 Deliver a weekly Monday Morning Executive MIS report detailing section-wise attendance, assessment percentiles, and remediation needs to the Institution's TPO and Principal.  
3.5 Issue co-branded, digitally verifiable Certificates of Completion to all eligible students meeting the minimum 80% attendance and test submission criteria.  
3.6 Supply all requisite documentation and attendance logs formatted for the Institution’s **NBA Criterion 4/5** and **NAAC Criterion 5.1.3** audits.

---

### 4. ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE INSTITUTION
4.1 Provide adequate air-conditioned computer laboratories equipped with 1 functioning desktop/laptop per student, high-speed broadband internet (minimum 50 Mbps dedicated bandwidth), licensed/open-source compilers (Java 17+, Python 3.11+, GCC/G++), and high-resolution projectors with audio systems.  
4.2 Ensure 100% student punctuality, classroom discipline, and enforce mandatory student attendance (minimum 80% attendance requirement).  
4.3 Appoint a dedicated Faculty Placement Coordinator / Single Point of Contact (SPOC) to coordinate lab allocation, timetable alignment, and administrative liaison.  
4.4 Ensure timely clearance of commercial invoices as per the milestone payment schedule defined in Clause 5.

### 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS (IPR)
6.1 All pedagogical content, lecture slides, coding problem statements, software test platforms, algorithms, and training materials supplied by CampusEdge remain the exclusive intellectual property of CampusEdge EdTech Private Limited.  
6.2 The Institution and its students are granted a non-exclusive, non-transferable revocable license to utilize the materials solely for their personal academic learning during the duration of the course. No materials shall be recorded, redistributed, or commercialized without prior written consent.

---

### 7. CONFIDENTIALITY & DATA PROTECTION
7.1 Both Parties agree to maintain strict confidentiality regarding all student personal data, commercial terms, proprietary test scores, and institutional statistics.  
7.2 Student information shall be processed strictly in accordance with India's **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023** solely for training, assessment, and placement facilitation purposes.

---

### 8. TERM & TERMINATION
8.1 This MoU shall come into effect on the date of execution and shall remain valid for a period of **one (1) academic year**, renewable automatically upon mutual written consent.  
8.2 Either Party may terminate this MoU by providing thirty (30) days prior written notice in the event of a material breach not cured within fifteen (15) days of receipt of notice. In the event of early termination, the Institution shall compensate CampusEdge on a pro-rata basis for all hours delivered up to the effective termination date.
