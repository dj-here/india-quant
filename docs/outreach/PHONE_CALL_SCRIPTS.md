# CampusEdge Institutional Phone Calling Playbook

This playbook provides exact conversational dialogues for sales representatives reaching out to Training & Placement Officers (TPOs), Department HoDs, and Principals across Indian colleges.

---

## 1. Navigating the Campus Gatekeeper (Operator / Department Clerk)

**Goal**: Transfer from the college main landline/reception directly to the TPO or obtain their direct mobile number.

- **Representative**: *"Good morning. Please connect me to the Head of Training & Placement, Professor {{TPO_LAST_NAME}}'s desk, please."*
- **Gatekeeper**: *"May I know who is calling and regarding what?"*
- **Representative**: *"This is {{SENDER_NAME}} calling from CampusEdge Bangalore. We are scheduling the state technical campus diagnostic assessments for the 2026/27 engineering batch. Professor {{TPO_LAST_NAME}} requested the placement benchmarking dossier. Is he in his cabin right now?"*
- **Gatekeeper**: *"Sir is in a meeting / taking a lecture."*
- **Representative**: *"Understood. Should I reach him directly on his mobile or his placement cell extension? I have his email and just need to confirm his availability for 2 minutes."*

---

## 2. Cold Call Conversation Flow with TPO

### Step 1: Respectful & High-Impact Hook (First 15 Seconds)
- **Representative**: *"Good morning, Professor {{TPO_LAST_NAME}} Sir. I am {{SENDER_NAME}} from CampusEdge Learning Solutions. I know you are in the middle of a busy academic day, so I will be very brief. Do you have 60 seconds?"*
- **TPO**: *"Yes, tell me quickly, what is this regarding?"*
- **Representative**: *"Sir, we have analyzed recent campus hiring tests for TCS Prime, Cognizant GenC Next, and product firms in {{STATE}}. Over 65% of pre-final year students are getting eliminated in the first 60-minute online coding & aptitude round because modern tests now evaluate dynamic sliding window and tree algorithms rather than basic syntax. 
  
  At CampusEdge, our ex-FAANG and industry trainers deliver hands-on DSA (Java/Python/C++) and speed aptitude that have doubled Day-1 assessment clearances for institutions like {{PEER_COLLEGE}}. We want to offer {{COLLEGE_NAME}} a **Free 90-Minute Placement Diagnostic Masterclass** or a free 20-question readiness assessment for your 3rd-year cohort. Have you finalized your external placement training calendar for this semester yet?"*

---

### Step 2: Diagnostic Discovery Questions
1. *"Sir, roughly how many students from your 2026/27 batch are eligible for technical placements this season?"*
2. *"In your previous drives, which round was the biggest filter—was it the online coding test, the quantitative aptitude, or the technical interview?"*
3. *"Do you currently have an external training partner conducting full-semester training, or is it handled in-house by faculty?"*

---

### Step 3: Overcoming Common Phone Brush-Offs

#### Brush-off A: "Just send an email with your brochure."
- **Representative**: *"I will certainly send our complete 1-page brochure to your email right now, Professor {{LAST_NAME}}. 
  
  However, since college inboxes receive dozens of brochures daily, I would love to show you a 3-minute live view of our real-time TPO analytics dashboard and our week-by-week DSA syllabus on a quick Zoom call. 
  
  Would tomorrow at 11:30 AM or Thursday at 3:00 PM work better for a brief 12-minute screen share?"*

#### Brush-off B: "We already have an existing training vendor / We are happy with them."
- **Representative**: *"That is great to hear, Sir—most forward-thinking colleges like {{COLLEGE_NAME}} already have training arrangements. We certainly do not ask you to replace them. 
  
  However, many of our current partner colleges also had existing vendors. What they found was that while older vendors teach theoretical concepts, students were failing the live competitive coding rounds. 
  
  Why not give CampusEdge just one challenging batch or conduct our **Free Student Readiness Assessment** alongside your existing vendor's students? You can compare the score jumps and student feedback directly. Would you be open to a 2-week pilot with zero risk?"*

#### Brush-off C: "We do not have any budget from management."
- **Representative**: *"We completely understand institutional budget cycles, Sir. That is why our **2-Week Placement Kickstart Pilot** is offered at a subsidized fee of just ₹25,000 for 60 students—and 100% of that pilot fee is credited back against your semester contract if you choose to partner with us later. 
  
  Alternatively, our 90-Minute Placement Masterclass is 100% complimentary. Can I book a 15-minute slot on Zoom to present the diagnostic report structure to you and the Principal?"*

---

### Step 4: Closing the Call & Securing the Meeting
- **Representative**: *"Wonderful, Professor {{LAST_NAME}}. I have sent the calendar invite to `{{TPO_EMAIL}}` for Thursday at 11:30 AM. I will also send a quick WhatsApp confirmation to your mobile with our company profile. Thank you for your leadership, and we look forward to meeting you on Thursday!"*
