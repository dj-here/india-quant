# CampusEdge Sales Rep Battlecard: Quick-Reference Objection Handling

Print this one-page battlecard and keep it beside your phone during cold calling and college meetings.

---

| Objection Encountered | What the TPO / Principal Really Means | Your Winning Response Formula (Acknowledge → Reframe → Risk Reversal) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"We do not have any budget."** | *"I don't know if this delivers ROI and management will grill me on cost."* | 1. **Acknowledge**: *"Understood, Sir. College budgets are tightly governed."*<br>2. **Reframe**: *"When 25 more students secure 5+ LPA jobs, your admissions team uses that directly on billboards to fill 50+ paid seats next year."*<br>3. **Risk Reversal**: *"Our 2-Week Pilot is just ₹25,000 for 60 students—and 100% of it is credited against your semester fee. You test us with zero budgetary risk."* |
| **"Our timetable is full; no room for extra classes."** | *"HoDs will complain about losing lecture hours for university exams."* | 1. **Acknowledge**: *"University syllabus adherence is critical."*<br>2. **Reframe**: *"We do not interfere with morning theory lectures. We deliver either: a) Friday/Saturday afternoon zero-hour slots, b) 2-hour evening hybrid labs, or c) A focused 10-day post-exam vacation bootcamp."*<br>3. **Value-Add**: *"Our training directly strengthens their university DSA practical exam performance as well."* |
| **"Our internal faculty already conducts training."** | *"I don't want to hurt faculty ego or admit an internal gap."* | 1. **Acknowledge**: *"Your faculty does fantastic work teaching core theory and university curriculum."*<br>2. **Reframe**: *"Modern tests like TCS Digital, Infosys SP, and Cognizant GenC test competitive programming tricks and sliding window optimizations outside standard textbooks."*<br>3. **Co-Existence**: *"Our ex-FAANG trainers serve as an industry finishing school alongside your professors. We even share all our problem banks with your department."* |
| **"We already have another vendor."** | *"Switching is administrative friction; I am comfortable with my current contact."* | 1. **Acknowledge**: *"That's great—most top colleges already have training."*<br>2. **Reframe**: *"Are you 100% satisfied with their Day-1 online test clearance rates and daily attendance?"*<br>3. **Challenge/Test**: *"Don't cancel your current vendor. Let CampusEdge run our Free Diagnostic Assessment or a 2-Week Pilot on just one batch. Compare student scores and attendance head-to-head."* |
| **"Students lose interest and stop coming after 1 week."** | *"Previous vendors were boring; students bunked classes and wasted our money."* | 1. **Acknowledge**: *"Slide-heavy lectures bore students immediately."*<br>2. **Reframe**: *"CampusEdge uses a 20-80 active coding model: 20 minutes theory, 80 minutes live competitive coding with real-time class leaderboards."*<br>3. **Guarantee**: *"Our automated WhatsApp tracking alerts parents and faculty instantly. We maintain a verified 90%+ attendance rate across 40+ colleges."* |
| **"Send your proposal via email; we will review it."** | *"I am brushing you off because I am busy right now."* | 1. **Acknowledge**: *"I am emailing the 1-page brochure to you right now, Sir."*<br>2. **Micro-Commitment**: *"College inboxes get 50 brochures daily. Can I take just 10 minutes on Zoom on Thursday at 11:30 AM to show you our live student analytics dashboard? It takes 10 minutes and gives you the exact competitive benchmarks."* |
